Launch-Day Checklist

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print this on launch day (or the night before) and keep it on your desk. It's organized from morning to evening: prepare, go live, steady the ship, then close kindly. You do NOT need to do everything — the sheet's job is to stop you from spinning. Tick boxes as you go. The evening section exists to remind you to stop working.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/launch-day-checklist.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
