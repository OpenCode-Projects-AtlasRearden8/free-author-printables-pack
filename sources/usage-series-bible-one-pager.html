Series Bible One-Pager

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print one per series and keep it beside your keyboard. Fill in planned books first, then your main cast, places, and the timeline rules you must not contradict. The 'things that must never happen' box is gold: note the retcons you've already decided against. Update in pencil as the series grows — this is a living sheet.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/series-bible-one-pager.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
