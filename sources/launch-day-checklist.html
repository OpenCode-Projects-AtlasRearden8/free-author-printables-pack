Launch-Day Checklist

One page, top to bottom. If it's not on this list, it can wait until tomorrow.

Book: Date:

Morning: before anything goes live

  • Confirm book is priced correctly everywhere it's sold (spot-check the live buy page)
  • Reread the store description — fix any typo you spot
  • Author page / bio links point to the new book
  • Email to your list is loaded and scheduled for your usual send time

Midday: go live and be present

  • Publish the "it's live" post on your main platform
  • Ask your inner circle to share or repost (one friendly ask, not three)
  • Screenshot or save the live product link for future posts
  • Reply to every early comment (fast, warm, human)

Afternoon: steady the ship

  • Post again with a behind-the-scenes angle (why this book exists)
  • Log actual sales numbers in the sales log sheet
  • Check your supply of thank-you cards and bookmarks for early buyers
  • Set a hard stop time to log off

Evening: close kindly

  • Send thank-you notes to whoever helped today
  • Write 3 sentences: what went well, what to change, how you feel
  • Approved to open the champagne (or fizzy water)
