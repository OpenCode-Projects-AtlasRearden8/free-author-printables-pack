Review Request Postcards

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print on plain cardstock (US Letter), two postcards per page, cut along the dashed line. The front/side with blanks is where the buyer adds their own address and their own note — leave those fields empty. The printed ask is short on purpose: honest review, one sentence, no pressure. Great to hand out with signed copies.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/review-request-postcards.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
