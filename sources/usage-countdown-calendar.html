Launch-Week Countdown Calendar

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print this the week BEFORE your book launches. Write your book title and launch date at the top, then do one focus area per day. The blanks under each day are for whatever you actually did, which is a nicer record than what you planned. If a day falls apart, use the 'one task I will not skip' box at the bottom to make a promise to yourself that's small enough to keep.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/countdown-calendar.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
