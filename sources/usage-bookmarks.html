Bookmark Templates

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print on cardstock (65–80 lb) on US Letter. Cut along the dashed line — there are two bookmarks per page. Fill in the blanks with pen: book title, your name, a favorite quote, which book of the series this is, and when Book 2 is coming if it's useful. They tuck perfectly into a sold copy or a giveaway envelope.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/bookmarks.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
