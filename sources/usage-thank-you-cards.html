Thank-You Card Templates

A friendly guide to this printable sheet.

Print on plain paper (US Letter). There are three quarter-fold cards per page: crop or cut along the dashed line, fold in half twice, and you have a tiny card. The blank lines are for the buyer's name and a personal sentence — two handwritten words beat a paragraph of print. Use any card on the sheet; they're all the same size.

What you need

  • The sheet's own PDF (printables/thank-you-cards.html)
  • US Letter paper — regular printer is fine (cardstock for bookmarks)
  • A pen you like
Troubleshooting: if the sheet prints oversized, pick "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") in the print dialog. Every sheet fits US Letter with 1/2-inch margins, which every home printer can show.
